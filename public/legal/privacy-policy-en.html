  • device type, model, operating system version, and app version

In the web version, we use cookies and similar technologies for authentication, session continuity, and the proper operation of the Service. You may restrict cookies in your browser settings, but some features may not function correctly as a result.

3.4. Voice Input

Certain features may allow voice input. Audio data may be processed by the device operating system or by third-party speech recognition services used by the user's device. In Mentala, we use the speech recognition result in text form. We do not store voice recordings as a separate category of data.

4. Purposes of Processing

  • user registration and account management
  • provision of the Service features, including meditations, habit tools, and personalization
  • operation of the digital assistant and generation of assistant responses
  • processing of response feedback in order to investigate issues and improve answer quality
  • ensuring security, preventing abuse, and protecting infrastructure
  • processing user requests and providing support
  • improving the quality of the Service based on aggregated and technical data

5. Marketing Communications and Separate Consent

We may send service-related messages that are necessary for the operation of the Service, such as confirmations, security alerts, and notices of changes to the terms.

Advertising and marketing communications, including emails about new features, discounts, promotions, and promotional notifications, are sent only on the basis of the user's separate consent. Such consent may be withdrawn at any time through the settings or by contacting support.

6. Special Categories of Personal Data

In chat interactions and certain self-help scenarios, users may voluntarily share information about their well-being, anxiety, mood, and emotional condition. Such information may qualify as special categories of personal data under applicable data protection laws. We process such data only for the purpose of providing the Service functionality and only with the user's consent.

If you do not want such information to be processed, do not enter it in chat and do not use scenarios that require you to describe your condition.

7. Chat Memory and Storage

Mentala may include memory and conversational context features. In particular:

  • contextual continuity of the conversation on the side of technological components that help generate a response
  • transfer of the user's message text, active session context, and technical request data to a third-party AI provider in order to generate a response from the digital assistant
  • a session summary that may be stored in our database on servers located in the Russian Federation for personalization within Mentala

We recommend that you do not include excessive personal information in chat, including passport details, banking information, exact home address, or other information that is not necessary for the operation of the Service.

For feedback on assistant responses, we store a minimal set of data: the response identifier, rating, selected reason, and technical request context, such as platform, time zone, and request-id. Text comments submitted in feedback are stored for a limited period and are automatically deleted after 60 days, while the rating record itself may be retained for service quality analytics.

Chat messages and related context may be stored for the duration of the user's account use unless they are deleted earlier by the user or at the user's request. After the account is deleted, such data is deleted or anonymized unless otherwise required by law.

Technical logs related to digital assistant request processing may be retained for a limited period to the extent necessary for security, error investigation, abuse prevention, and the stable operation of the Service.

8. Where We Store Data and Data Localization

Personal data of users who are citizens of the Russian Federation is subject to primary recording, systematization, accumulation, storage, clarification, and retrieval using databases located within the Russian Federation, where required by law. Our main database, session summaries, and server logs are hosted on infrastructure located in the Russian Federation.

9. Sharing with Third Parties and Cross-Border Transfers

Certain components of the Service may rely on third-party technology and cloud providers. This may include push delivery, infrastructure, payment processing, and digital assistant functionality.

  • push notification providers, such as Apple Push Notification service (APNs) and Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM)
  • infrastructure, cloud hosting, storage, and data protection providers
  • payment infrastructure providers when payments are made through a web page
  • the third-party AI provider OpenAI, if used to generate digital assistant responses

Data is shared with such providers only to the minimum extent necessary for them to perform the relevant functions.

If a user interacts with the digital assistant, the text of the user's messages, the relevant active session context, the language of the request, and the technical data necessary to process the request and deliver the response may be transferred to the third-party AI provider OpenAI solely for the purpose of generating a response from the digital assistant.

The transfer of data to the third-party AI provider for chat functionality takes place only after an explicit user action and a confirmed consent flow within the application interface. If the user does not provide such consent, the AI chat function is not activated and no data is transferred for response generation.

We do not use the content of users' chat messages to train or fine-tune our own models. Any use of data by the third-party AI provider is governed by that provider's data processing terms and the agreements applicable to the integration we use.

We confirm that any third-party service providers, including the AI provider OpenAI, to whom users' personal data is transferred, maintain a level of data protection that is equivalent to or no less protective than the safeguards described in this Policy.

Cross-border transfers may take place, including to countries that provide an adequate level of protection for data subjects' rights, or on the basis of the user's consent where required.

Cross-border transfers are carried out where there is a valid legal basis and in compliance with the procedures required under applicable law.

10. Payments and Subscriptions

When a subscription is paid for through a web page, payment data is processed by the payment infrastructure provider. We do not receive or store full bank card details. We receive only the information necessary to confirm payment, account for the subscription, and perform our obligations, for example payment status and transaction identifier.

If payment is made through application stores, payment processing is governed by the rules of the relevant platform.

11. Security Measures

We apply organizational and technical measures designed to protect personal data, including:

  • encryption of data transmission using secure protocols
  • access controls and the principle of least privilege
  • protection of server infrastructure and regular updates
  • backup and recovery procedures
  • restricted access to secrets and encryption keys

No method of storing or transmitting data can guarantee absolute security. If an incident is identified, we act in accordance with our internal procedures and the requirements of applicable law.

12. Retention Periods

We retain personal data for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes of processing and providing the Service, or for the periods required by law.

  • account data is retained for the period during which the Service is used and until the user deletes the account, unless a longer retention period is required by law
  • chat messages and related contextual data are retained for the period necessary to support chat functionality and personalization, until deleted by the user or at the user's request, unless otherwise required by law
  • text comments submitted in feedback on assistant responses are automatically deleted after 60 days unless longer retention is necessary for abuse investigations or compliance with legal obligations
  • technical logs and diagnostic data are retained for a limited period to the extent necessary for security, diagnostics, and the stable operation of the Service

12.1. How to Delete Data (Data Deletion Request)

You can delete your data in the following ways:

  • delete your account in the application profile settings
  • send a deletion request to support at support@mentala.app

Data will be deleted unless retention is required by law.
