3. What data we collect

3.1. Data you provide

  • email address
  • name or pseudonym, if you provide it
  • profile settings and preferences
  • data about the use of meditation and practice features, for example the selected track, timer, favorites
  • messages and content in the chat with the digital assistant, if you use the chat
  • response feedback data for assistant answers: like/dislike rating, selected reason, and optional comment when you provide it
  • support requests

3.2. Data collected automatically

  • IP address and technical connection parameters
  • device type, model, operating system version, application version
  • technical identifiers necessary for the operation of the application, including notification identifiers
  • technical event logs and error diagnostics within the scope of ensuring operability

3.3. Cookies and similar technologies for the web version

In the web version, we use cookies and similar technologies for authorization, maintaining the session, and the correct operation of the Service. You can restrict cookies in your browser settings, but some functions may not work properly.

3.4. Voice input

Voice input may be available in certain features. Audio data may be processed by the operating system of the user's device or by third-party speech recognition services used by the user's device. In Mentala, we use the recognition result in the form of text. We do not store voice input audio recordings as a separate type of data.

4. Purposes of data processing

  • registration and account management
  • providing features of the Service, including meditations, habits, and personalization
  • operation of the digital assistant and generation of responses
  • processing response feedback to investigate issues and improve answer quality
  • ensuring security, preventing abuse, and protecting infrastructure
  • processing requests and technical support
  • improving the quality of the Service based on aggregated and technical data

5. Marketing communications and separate consent

We may send service-related messages that are necessary for the operation of the Service, such as confirmations, security alerts, and changes to terms.

Advertising and marketing communications, including emails about new features, discounts and promotions, as well as promotional notifications, are sent only with the user's separate consent. Consent may be withdrawn at any time via settings or by submitting a request to support.

6. Special categories of personal data

In the chat and some self-help scenarios, the user may voluntarily provide information about well-being, anxiety, mood, and psycho-emotional state. Such information may relate to special categories of personal data within the meaning of personal data legislation. We process it only for the purpose of providing features of the Service and only with the user's consent.

If you do not want such information to be processed, do not enter it in the chat and do not use scenarios where it is necessary to describe your state.

7. Memory and storage of chat data

Mentala may provide memory modes and conversation context. In particular:

  • a contextual continuation mode on the side of technological components that help generate the response
  • transmission of the user's message text, active session context, and technical request data to a third-party AI provider for generating the digital assistant's response
  • a session summary that may be stored in our database on servers in the Russian Federation for personalization within Mentala

We recommend that you do not provide excessive personal information in the chat, including passport details, bank details, an exact home address, and other data that is not required for the operation of the Service.

For assistant response feedback, we store a minimal data set: assistant response identifier, rating, selected topic, and technical request context (platform, timezone, request-id). The textual feedback comment is retained for a limited period and is automatically cleared after 60 days, while the rating record itself may remain for quality analytics.

Chat messages and related context may be retained for the lifetime of the user's account unless they are deleted earlier by the user or upon the user's request. After account deletion, such data is deleted or anonymized unless otherwise required by law.

Technical logs related to digital assistant requests may be retained for a limited period to the extent necessary for security, error investigation, abuse prevention, and stable operation of the Service.

8. Where we store data; localization

Personal data of users who are citizens of the Russian Federation is subject to initial recording, systematization, accumulation, storage, clarification, and retrieval using databases located in the territory of the Russian Federation. The main database, session summaries, and server logs are hosted on infrastructure located in the Russian Federation.

9. Disclosure to third parties and cross-border transfer

Third-party technological and cloud providers may be used for the operation of certain Service components. This may include authorization functions, delivery of notifications, and operation of the digital assistant.

  • notification delivery providers, for example Apple Push Notification service (APNs) and Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM)
  • authorization and infrastructure providers, including cloud infrastructure and hosting
  • payment infrastructure providers for payments made via the web page
  • digital assistant technology providers, if they are used to generate responses

Data is disclosed to such providers to the minimum extent necessary and only to perform the specified functions. Some of these providers may be located outside the Russian Federation, which may constitute a cross-border transfer.

If the user uses the digital assistant, the text of their messages, the related context of the active session, the language of the request, and the technical data necessary to process the request and deliver the response may be transferred to the third-party AI provider OpenAI exclusively for generating the digital assistant's response.

Transfer of data to a third-party AI provider for the operation of the chat takes place only after the user's explicit action and confirmation of consent in the application interface. If the user does not provide such consent, the AI chat function is not activated and data for generating a response is not transferred.

We do not use the content of the user's messages in the chat to train or fine-tune our own models. The third-party AI provider's use of data is governed by its data processing terms and the agreements applicable to the integration we use.

We confirm that any third-party service providers, including the AI provider OpenAI, to whom users' personal data is transferred, provide a level of data protection equivalent to or equal to the safeguards described in this Policy.

Cross-border transfer may be carried out, among other things, to countries that ensure adequate protection of the rights of personal data subjects or with the user's consent.

Cross-border transfer is carried out on legal grounds and in compliance with the procedures provided for by applicable law. The operator notifies Roskomnadzor of the commencement of cross-border transfer of personal data prior to such transfer in cases where this is required by the legislation of the Russian Federation.

11. Security measures

We apply organizational and technical measures to protect personal data, including:

  • encryption of data transmission via secure protocols
  • access control and the principle of least privilege
  • protection of server infrastructure and regular component updates
  • backups and recovery procedures
  • access control to secrets and keys

No method of storage or transmission of data can guarantee absolute security. If incidents are identified, we act in accordance with internal procedures and the requirements of applicable law.

12. Retention periods

We retain personal data for as long as necessary for the purposes of processing and providing the Service, or for the periods required by law.

  • account data is retained for the period of use of the Service and until the account is deleted by the user, unless otherwise required by law

  • feedback comments on assistant responses are automatically cleared after 60 days unless longer retention is required for investigating abuse or complying with legal requirements
